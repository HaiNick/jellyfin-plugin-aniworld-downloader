AniWorld Downloader

Connection

As the Jellyfin server reaches it. Phones never call it directly.
Create one in AniWorld Downloader under Settings → API Keys. A key with "Read and download" access is enough. It stays on this server.
A second key with "Full access". With it, admins get an Auto-Sync page next to the queue that lists every tracked series and can edit, check or remove them. Leave it empty to hide that page.

Downloads

Used when a title offers it; users can still pick another one.
Finished downloads show up in Jellyfin within a minute instead of at the next scheduled scan. A series already in the library gets only its own folder scanned.

Who may download

Everyone who is logged in may search. Admins may always download; tick the other users who may too.
